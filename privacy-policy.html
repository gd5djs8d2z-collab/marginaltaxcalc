Privacy Policy

How ontariomarginaltaxcalc.ca handles your information.

Last updated: June 2026

1. About This Policy

This privacy policy explains how ontariomarginaltaxcalc.ca collects, uses, and protects information when you visit and use our website.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

ontariomarginaltaxcalc.ca does not collect, store, or transmit any of the income figures you enter into the calculator. All calculations are performed locally in your browser using JavaScript. No form data is sent to our servers.

3. Calculator Data

The numbers you enter into the calculator remain entirely in your browser. We have no access to your inputs, results, or any personal financial information. Nothing is logged, stored, or transmitted.

4. Third-Party Services

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) for traffic analysis and Google AdSense for advertising. These services may collect anonymous usage data. You can opt out of GA4 tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Cookies and Advertising

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics and Google AdSense. We do not set any first-party cookies ourselves. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, which will prevent analytics and advertising cookies from being set.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also disable cookies in your browser. This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
